<h1>Introduction
    Frog Hopper is an online action game for mobiles and PC's.

    The game play consists of making a frog hop from the base of the
    display area to the entrance sprite at the top of the display area.

    The frog hops from lily pad to lily pad on the surface of a pond.

    The player controls the hopping with an arc-shaped control, a touch
    on which sets the direction and extent of the leap.

    If the frog lands in the water rather than on a lily pad, the
    attempt for that frog is failed and the user is presented with
    a new frog on a lily pad at the base of the display area, with
    the lily pads re-arranged.

    There is a 25% chance that the frog will be intercepted by a leaping
    fish during one of its leaps. In this case the frog is lost and
    play is restarted with a new frog, as for falling in the water.

    5 points are scored for each successful hop. When the frog
    reaches the entrance at the top of the display, 20 points are scored
    plus 1 point for each five seconds less than 1 minute 30 seconds to get 
    from start to finish (entrance).

    There are twelve frog attempts in each game. 

    When all the frog attempts have been used, the end of game score
    chart is displayed, showing the number of completed runs and
    the total score.

<h1>Display Layout
    The display is centred with a width of 360px for mobiles and 500px
    for PC's.

<h2>Introductory Panel
    The Introductory Panel appears as an overlay when the game is
    first loaded. It provides a description of the game and the use
    of the hop control on the right hand side of the base of the
    display.

<h2>Menu Bar
    The menu bar is displayed at the top of the display. It consists
    of a logo at the left and Charts, Restart and Home buttons on the
    right. 
    
    The Charts button causes action to be suspended, while
    the overlay for the last (highest) up to ten scores of the current 
    session are displayed.

    The Restart button causes the game to be restarted.

    The Home button causes the browser to display the home page
    at ../index.html.

<h2>Stage
    The game takes place on the Konva stage. This either 360px wide
    for mobiles or 500px wide for PC's x 500px high.

<h3>Background
    The background of the stage is greyish blue, representing the
    pool. The top 60 pixels are cyan/blue representing the sky
    and horizon.

<h3>Entrance
    The reed entrance sprite used to complete a game round is 80px 
    wide and set at the top of the stage. It is offset between 20px 
    from the left of the stage and 100px from the right.

<h3>Hop Control
    The hop control is a circular arc curved area, ranging from
    -120 to +120 degrees from the vertical. It is shaded from
    medium grey at its centre to white at its outer edge. The 
    radius of the arc circle is 60px. Touching/clicking the 
    control at any point in the arc sets the extent of the hop
    and the angle from the display vertical. The extent of the hop 
    ranges from 20px near the centre to 140px at its outer arc edge.

    The hop control sits at the base of the stage, on the right, with
    a gap of 5px from the stage edges.

<h3>Lily Pads
    The lily pads are arranged in rows of random 1 to 3 (360px stage) or
    1 to 5 (500px stage). They are 60px wide. The sprite is lilyPad.png. 
    
    The first row has the lily pad top at 90px from the stage base, 
    but the pads do not touch or cover the hop control on the right.
    The subsequent rows are 80px apart, with the vertical position
    of the lily pads varying by +/-10px random from the row.

    There should always be at least one instance of a distance of
    140px or less between lily pads from row to row.

    There are 5 rows of lily pads in total.

<h3>Frog
    There is only ever one frog on the stage, which begins on the first
    (bottom) lily pad row of the stage. It always sits at the centre of a 
    lily pad, but faces in the last direction hopped.

    The sitting frog has 6 sprites facing right of vertical at intervals
    of 22.5 degrees (0 to 112.5 degrees). They are named frog01.png
    to frog06.png respectively. The sprite displayed is the nearest angle
    to the hop angle. The sprites can be flipped to give the left angles.
    The sitting frog is displayed 40px wide.

    When the frog hops, the frog is briefly shown halfway along its
    trajectory with legs extended. The sprites for this are frogLeaping01.png
    to frogLeaping06.png at the same angles as the sitting frog (22.5 degree 
    steps). These also can be flipped for the left going angles.

    The width of the leaping sprite ranges from 40px at 0 degrees, to 60px at
    90 degrees.

    If the leaping frog does not land with its centre within 50 pixels of the
    centre of the lily pad it is lost, and the splash sprite, splash.png is briefly
    displayed (40px wide, at the trajectory midpoint).

<h3>Fish 
    If the frog is leaping from row to row (ie: more than 20 degrees from the 
    horizontal) then there is a 25% chance that it will be intercepted
    by a fish. This appears as the sprite leapingFish.png, coming from the
    right or left of the frog as it reaches its trajectory midpoint.

    The splash sprite splash.png is displayed at the trajectory midpoint briefly
    after the fish.

    The fish faces right in the file, so can be flipped to have it coming from 
    the right (facing left).

<h2>Status Lines
    These appear below the stage.

    The status lines are used to indicate when a frog has been lost, or reached
    the entrance. They also display the current score.

<h2>Game End Overlay
    The game end overlay is displayed when the twelfth frog reaches the
    entrance or is lost. It displays the total score and the number of 
    rounds completed successfully along with the game ended message.

    It has a Restart button, which clears the overlay and restarts the game.

<h2>Charts Overlay
    The charts overlay suspends play and displays the up to ten top
    scoring games as points and rounds completed in the current
    browser session.

    The overlay has a Dismiss button, which clears the overlay and
    resumes the game.
